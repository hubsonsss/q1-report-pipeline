## 2. Data mismatch: CRM vs commissioning sign-offs ⚠️

Claire presented the reconciliation she flagged in Week 13. **The March numbers in the CRM do not reconcile with the commissioning sign-off system.** Since getting CRM access on 3 March, the VoltWorks crew has been entering their completed jobs — but some of the same installations were entered in parallel by our sales reps when closing the contracts.
